# Freelancer & Small Business Finance Tracker (US)

Excel / Google Sheets template for US freelancers and small businesses: income and expense ledgers,
monthly summary, tax set-aside estimate, unpaid-invoice tracking and a dashboard with charts.

Rebuild the file with `python3 build_tracker.py` (needs `openpyxl`). Output: `Freelancer_Finance_Tracker.xlsx`.
